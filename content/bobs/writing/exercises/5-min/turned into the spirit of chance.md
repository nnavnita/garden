# Turned into the Spirit of Chance
## [[ bobs/reading/reading list | The Female Man - Joanna Russ]]

They say that life is the culmination of a series of happenstance, or was it the result of a series of unlikely events? I forget. Regardless, I've lived long enough (three decades, now!) to see the truth in that statement. At the very least, the perspective behind it seems to mirror my own -- what would my life look like today if I made a different choice, what if I woke up slightly earlier, what if I decided to eat something slightly different, what if I said what I wanted to say with the right words? This moment right here, the one that has me penning down these thoughts while sat in this library is the result of all those small decisions, each one a promise of an experience, each one turned into the spirit of chance.
