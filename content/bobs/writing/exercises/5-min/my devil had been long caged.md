# My Devil had been Long Caged
## [[ bobs/reading/reading list | The Strange Case of Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde - Robert Louis Stevenson ]]

I've learnt to hold my tongue and mince my words. I've learnt to pick my battles and save my breath. I've developed the skin of a chameleon to put up with your colours, so well you'd think I was born with them. I've tried all I could to fit into your mould, to earn your favour and good graces. But only once you departed without a backwards turn did I realise that while my devil had been long caged, it took with it my own self too.
