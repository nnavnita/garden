title: writing
---

A really fun [[bobs/writing/exercises/5-min | writing exercise]] my dad taught me was to open a book and pick a phrase at random, usually the first few words my eyes land on, then give myself five minutes to write whatever thoughts the phrase provokes freeform.

I've found this a really good way to combat writer's block and whenever I visit a bookstore or library, I make it a point to find an interesting book (whose title or cover speaks to me) and perform this exercise. I find that it has quite a few benefits: 
- it helps me get my creative juices flowing and write uninhibited since the short clock doesn't allow me much time to be self-conscious
- it allows me to think about topics I might never have otherwise and thus broaden my horizons
- it has the side benefit of providing me with a [[reading list]] of books that have caught my eye
