# Mutton Kassa

## Ingredients

### For Masala Grinding
- 3 tsp dhaniya seeds
- 2 tsp jeera seeds
- 1 tsp saunf
- 4 elaichi
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 cloves
- 1/2 tsp pepper
- 3 medium-sized onions
- 3-4 tomatoes (optional)
- 3 tsp ginger garlic

### For boiling
- 1kg mutton
- 1 bayleaf
- 1 tsp haldi
- 1 tsp salt

## Recipe for kassa
- Heat oil and fry green chillis, then add the paste and cook through
- Add mutton pieces and stir until well coated, fresh coriander to garnish
- Optionally add mutton stock from the boil if you want gravy
