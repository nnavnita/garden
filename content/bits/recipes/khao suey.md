# Khao Suey

## Ingredients
- Chicken breast
- Ginger
- S&P
- Garlic
- Onions
- Spring onions
- Fresh Coriander
- Lemon
- Kashmiri Chilli powder
- Chilli powder
- Coriander powder
- Jeera powder
- Coconut milk
- Egg noodles

## Recipe
- Boil chicken in some water with a pinch of salt, pepper and bruised ginger
- Shred cooked chicken
- Fry garlic slivers and onions for toppings, set aside
- In the same oil, add ginger garlic paste, 1/4 tsp haldi, 1/2tsp kashmiri chilli powder, 1tsp normal chilli powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp jeera powder
- Throw in chicken and add salt 1/4 tsp
- Pour coconut milk and the stock, let it come to a boil
- Cook your egg noodles acc package instructions
- Additional toppings:chopped roasted peanuts, spring onions, fresh coriander, sesame seads, squeeze of lemon
