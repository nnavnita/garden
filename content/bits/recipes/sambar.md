# Sambar

## Ingredients
- Thoor daal
- Haldi
- Chilli powder
- Sambar powder
- Coconut/jaggery
- Sesame oil
- Jeera
- Onions
- Garlic
- Green chillies
- Veggies
- Curry leaves
- Coriander
- Tamarind

## Recipe

- Pressure cook thoor dal with 1/2 tsp haldi and 1tsp oil
- In another vessel, add oil with jeera then onions then garlic, green chillies and chilli powder then haldi
- Add veggies and curry leaves, stir after 5min add one cup of water
- Lid on and boil the veggies for 10min, then add tamarind water/paste
- Add sambar powder and optionally coconut or jaggery
- Add required water to adjust the thickness
- Add salt to taste and sprinkle with fresh coriander leaves
