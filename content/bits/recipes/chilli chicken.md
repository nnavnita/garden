# Chilli Chicken

## Ingredients
- Dark soy sauce
- onion
- Green chillies
- Chicken
- Oil
- Garlic
- White pepper
- Cornstarch (optional)

## Recipe
- 5-10min boil chicken
- In 2tsp oil, fry garlic and onions (DON'T BROWN)
- Throw in the chicken, ginger and green chillies (8-12 depending on how spicy you want it)
- Add soy sauce (2-3tsp)
- Throw in the stock water with salt and white pepper
- Stir in the slurry at the end
- You can add capsicum and onion petals if you want + other asian sauces for variety
