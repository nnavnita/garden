# Consumers without Problems
## [[ hobbies/reading/reading list | Tea: How Tradition Stood in the Way of the Perfect Cup - Ian Bersten]]

They don't exist. It's as simple as that. By definition, a consumer is one who _consumes_. And why does one consume? To solve a problem. Albeit, I'm playing fast and loose with the word "problem" here -- people often think a problem translates more into a "need" than a "want", but if you hold the world's knowledge in the palm of your hands (you do!) and still don't know what to do with yourself, _that's_ a problem. If you're bored enough to turn to mindless binging, the boredom is a problem, the binging a solution. It might not be the _best_ solution, granted, but it's a solution all the same. One we readily consume, because we all are, by definition, consumers WITH problems.
